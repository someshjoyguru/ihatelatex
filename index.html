IhateLaTeX — free private LaTeX resume builder and Overleaf alternative

IhateLaTeX is a free, no-sign-up LaTeX suite that runs in your browser. Build an ATS-friendly resume or CV, a research paper, booktabs tables, and math formulas. PDFs compile on your device with WebAssembly TeX — files are not uploaded to compile.

Open the product: https://ihatelatex.com/
Resume builder: https://ihatelatex.com/latex-resume-builder/

Tools

  • Suite hub — all in-browser LaTeX tools
  • LaTeX resume & CV builder — Jake’s Resume, ATS checker
  • Research paper & thesis builder — IEEE / ACM / arXiv-style
  • LaTeX table generator — Excel/CSV to booktabs
  • Visual formula editor — draw, OCR, export LaTeX
  • Guides — ATS rules and builder comparisons

FAQ

Is it free? Yes — no account, no watermark.

Do I need LaTeX installed? No. Compilation is in the browser.

Is this an Overleaf alternative? Yes, for private on-device resumes and papers.
